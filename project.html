DEVELOPMENT VISION

Two Identical. One Mission.

We are designing and building two identical Formula Moto Student prototype motorcycles as per FMAE MotoStudent (India) regulations. Developing two identical machines lets us run parallel testing, compare performance data, and improve reliability — while reducing overall development time.

Engineered for track superiority

By using modular chromoly chassis structures and running digital multi-body dynamics validation, we ensure both the petrol and electric prototypes operate under identical vehicle geometry.
